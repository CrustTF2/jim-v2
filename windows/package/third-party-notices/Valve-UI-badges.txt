The Stat-Clock, Strange, and Unusual badge artwork is from Team Fortress 2 by
Valve. These PNGs are conversions of the installed game's materials/vgui/
viewmode_statclock.vtf, viewmode_strange.vtf, and viewmode_unusual.vtf textures.
See img/manager/badges/sources.json for the original texture hashes.

The display rule (Stat-Clock as the Strange variant for supported painted items,
Unusual above Strange) was checked against Valve's published item panel code:
https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/master/src/game/client/econ/item_model_panel.cpp
